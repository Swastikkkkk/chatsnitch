# Privacy policy

**ChatSnitch browser extension**

ChatSnitch does not collect, store, transmit or sell any data.

- It runs entirely inside your browser.
- It has no network access. Its content security policy blocks every outbound connection (`connect-src 'none'`).
- Its only permission is `management`, used to list your installed extensions, read their permissions, and turn off, turn on or remove an extension when you click the button to do so.
- It cannot read any website, including your AI chats.
- It keeps no history of scans.

The source code is public: https://github.com/Swastikkkkk/chatsnitch

**Website (chatsnitch.vercel.app)**

The website is separate from the extension. It records anonymous page visits and button clicks (page path, referring site and campaign tag) without cookies, IP addresses or any identifier. If you join the waitlist, your email address is stored only to send one message when the extension is available in the Chrome Web Store. To remove it, open an issue or contact the author via GitHub.

Contact: https://github.com/Swastikkkkk
