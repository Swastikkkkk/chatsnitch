# ChatSnitch launch video plan

**What it is:** a Chrome extension that shows which of your installed extensions can read your AI chats.
**For:** anyone who uses ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in Chrome and has installed a "free VPN" or AI sidebar at some point.
**What sets it apart:** security write-ups all say "audit your permissions manually". This does it in one click, and catches content-script access that Chrome's own API hides.
**Most striking claim (real, sourced):** extensions caught reading AI chats: 900K users (OX Security, Dec 2025), 8M+ installs (Koi Security / Malwarebytes, Dec 2025), 470K+ users (G DATA, Jun 2026).
**Visual hook:** Chrome's real install warning, "Read and change all your data on all websites", with the Add button getting clicked.
**Real UI shown:** the actual popup (same CSS), with the real scan result from the decoy test: 4 flagged, then Turn off on Free VPN Pro drops it to 3.
**Tone:** default, leaning serious. Punchy cuts, no jokes at the victims' expense.
**Format:** vertical 1080x1920 (reel), 30fps, 21s.
**Share caption:** That "Read and change all your data on all websites" box you clicked? It includes your AI chats. ChatSnitch shows which extensions can read them.

## Sound
Soft minor pad (Am, F, C, G) at 100 BPM with a muted pulse; a low thud on the Add click, soft ticks in key for each card, a short riser into the reveal, a gentle click on Turn off. Effects sit under the music.
